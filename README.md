## How I approach projects · 我的工作方式

- **Define the decision:** identify the user, the business question, and the outcome to evaluate.
- **Design the workflow:** make inputs, state changes, human decisions, and failure cases explicit.
- **Build and verify:** connect prototypes or analyses to inspectable outputs, tests, and documented limitations.

Tools used across these projects include Python, SQL/MySQL, PostgreSQL, FastAPI, Next.js, and statistical and machine-learning methods.

## Reading the evidence · 成果说明

The AI product cases describe prototypes and their documented validation, not claims of production adoption. Several research repositories are public reconstructions of coursework or research workflows; synthetic demonstrations are labelled, and restricted source data are excluded. Each repository explains its scope, data, and limitations.

AI-assisted implementation is part of my workflow. My focus is on problem definition, product and analytical choices, review, and verification; project-specific attribution is documented in the repositories.
